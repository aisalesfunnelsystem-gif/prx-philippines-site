The PRX platform How it works

Play. Confirm.
Move up.

No self-reported levels and no guesswork. Your PRX rating only moves when the people you played agree on what happened.

Every player has a verified PRX ID, Singles and Doubles are rated separately, and a Reliability score shows how much verified play sits behind each number.

FAQ Good questions

Good questions.

Is PRX free?

Yes. Recording matches, your PRX ratings, rankings, courts and open play are free for players. PRX Pro is optional and adds deeper insights.

When can I download the app?

PRX is in beta testing now and launches first in the Philippines. Follow PRX on Facebook to hear the moment it's available.

Why do I need to verify my identity?

So every rating belongs to one real person. It keeps rankings fair and stops duplicate accounts. See our Privacy Policy.

What is Reliability?

Reliability shows how much verified, confirmed play sits behind a rating. The more confirmed matches you play, the more reliable your rating becomes.

How do clubs and venues pay?

Create your club or venue in the app and you'll receive an email with a secure PayMongo payment link. Your plan activates automatically once payment goes through. You can also write to sales@prxworldwide.com.

How do I delete my account or data?

Request deletion inside the app or by email. See how account deletion works.
