How it works

Play. Confirm.
Move up.

No self-reported levels and no guesswork. Your rating only moves when the people you played agree on what happened.

Play your match

Singles or doubles, casual or at your club. Every player has a verified PRX ID, so you always know who you're up against.

Record & confirm

One player records the score in the app; the others confirm it. Only confirmed matches count.

Watch it move

Your PRX rating updates with a clear before, after and change, plus a Reliability score that shows how much verified play backs it up.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is PRX free?

Yes. Recording matches, your PRX ratings, rankings, courts and open play are free for players. PRX Pro is optional and adds deeper insights.

When can I download the app?

PRX is in beta testing now and launches first in the Philippines. Follow PRX on Facebook to hear the moment it's available.

Why do I need to verify my identity?

So every rating belongs to one real person. It keeps rankings fair and stops duplicate accounts. Verification is handled securely by our verification partner; see our Privacy Policy.

What is Reliability?

Reliability shows how much verified, confirmed play sits behind a rating. The more you play confirmed matches, the more reliable your rating becomes.

How do clubs and venues pay for Club Pro or Court Pro?

Create your club or venue in the app and you'll receive an email with a secure PayMongo payment link. Your plan activates automatically the moment payment goes through. You can also write to sales@prxworldwide.com.

How do I delete my account or data?

You can request deletion inside the app or by email. See how account deletion works.
