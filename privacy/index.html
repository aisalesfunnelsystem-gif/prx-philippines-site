Privacy notice

Pre-launch notice. This page describes the current PRX privacy approach for testing and pre-launch use. Final legal controller details and business address will be confirmed before public App Store release.

Last updated: 27 September 2026.

Information we collect

PRX Philippines may collect account and identity information such as your name, email address, mobile number and authentication details. Depending on the sport and features you use, PRX may also collect player profile details, match and verification records, ratings, reliability information, club or venue relationships, bookings, support communications and information you submit for account verification.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, operate player profiles, verify match results, calculate and display ratings and rankings, support clubs and venues, process bookings and payments, provide customer support, prevent abuse and fraud, improve product reliability and communicate important service information.

Competitive integrity

Paid subscriptions do not purchase or improve authoritative player ratings, reliability scores, leaderboard positions, match verification outcomes or competitive ranking priority. Competitive data is governed by the applicable PRX sport rules and verified activity.

Visibility and discovery

PRX may provide controls that let you manage discovery visibility and whether certain profile or club information is visible to other users. Some competition records may remain available where needed to preserve the integrity of historical results.

Clubs, venues and authorized partners

If you interact with a club, venue, event organizer or authorized federation partner, PRX may share information necessary to provide that service, verify participation, administer an event, manage access or comply with an authorized partnership.

Payments

PRX may use third-party payment providers for paid features, bookings or transactions. Full payment-card details do not need to be stored by PRX when the payment provider handles those credentials directly. Transaction and reconciliation records may be retained for support, accounting and dispute handling.

Service providers

PRX may use service providers for hosting, authentication, email, SMS delivery, analytics, payments, infrastructure and customer support. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services to PRX.

Data sale and advertising

PRX does not sell personal information to advertisers. If advertising or additional commercial data practices are introduced later, this notice will be updated before those practices are used.

Retention and account deletion

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, legal obligations, security, dispute resolution and the integrity of verified competitive records. You may request account deletion through the PRX app or by contacting privacy@prxphilippines.com. Limited non-identifying historical competition records may be retained where necessary to preserve the integrity of past verified matches and ratings, subject to applicable law.

Security

PRX uses technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect account and platform information. Security concerns can be reported to security@prxphilippines.com.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing of your personal information. Requests may be sent to privacy@prxphilippines.com.

Changes

PRX may update this notice as the platform evolves. Material changes will be reflected by an updated date and, where appropriate, additional notice in the app or website.
