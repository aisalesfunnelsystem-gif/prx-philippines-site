ACCOUNT DELETION

Delete your PRX account

This page explains how to delete your account in the PRX app (PRX Pickleball), published by GrowthPro System, and what happens to your data.

Option 1: In the PRX app

Open the PRX app, sign in, then go to Menu → Privacy & Security → Request account deletion and confirm. Your account is disabled and you are signed out straight away.

Option 2: By email (no app needed)

Send an email to privacy@prxworldwide.com from the email address on your PRX account, with the subject “PRX account deletion request”. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address before we process it. Never send passwords or one-time codes.

What is deleted

When the request is processed, PRX removes or anonymizes your name and other identity details, profile and photo, location, notifications, verification documents, active club memberships, private files and all sign-in information. You will no longer be able to sign in.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Match, rating and competition history is kept in non-identifying form, linked only to an internal player ID with no name or contact details, so past results and other players' ratings stay accurate.
  • A one-way verified-phone anti-abuse hash may be kept for up to 3 years to prevent abuse.
  • Payment and refund records may be kept as long as required for accounting, legal and dispute purposes.
  • Identity-verification data held by our verification provider (Didit): to ask for its deletion too, mention it in your email to privacy@prxworldwide.com.

Some information may be kept longer where the law requires it or where it is needed to resolve a dispute or prevent abuse. See our Privacy Policy for details.
